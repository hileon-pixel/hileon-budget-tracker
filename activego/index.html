What you can do

One place for the things you do together.

Activities are the core. Around them sit the communities, posts and tools that keep a group going between meetups.

Activities on a map

Everything happening nearby shows up as a pin. Filter by what you're into and see what's on around you.

Communities

Start or join groups around an interest, a place or a team. Each one has its own feed and members.

Posts and short video

Share a photo, write an update, or post a short clip to a vertical video feed.

Tickets

Hosts can issue tickets for an activity and scan them at the door with the in-app scanner.

Pro and business profiles

Verified accounts can run a storefront, promote a post, and sell tickets to what they host.

Messaging

Message the people you connect with and the groups you join, all in one inbox.

How it works

From "nothing to do" to out the door.

Step 1

Create or find

Post your own activity, or open the map to see what people have on nearby.

Step 2

Ask to join

Tap an activity and request a spot. The host decides who comes along.

Step 3

Meet up

Once you're approved you get the details. Then you show up, in person.

Meeting people, safely

You stay in control of who you meet.

Meeting people from an app means safety has to come first. These are built in, not optional.

  • You approve who joins. Activities you host only add people you accept.
  • Exact locations stay hidden. Others see an approximate area until you're accepted.
  • Block and report anywhere. Every profile, post and activity can be reported or blocked.
  • 16 and over. Active Go isn't built for children under 16, and asks for date of birth at sign-up.
