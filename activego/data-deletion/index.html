Data & account deletion

How to delete your Active Go account and the data tied to it.

On this page Delete in the app Delete by request What gets deleted What we keep Timing

You can delete your Active Go account and everything tied to it at any time. The quickest way is in the app; if you can't sign in, you can request deletion by email.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Active Go and go to your profile, then open Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and content are hidden right away and you're signed out.

Changed your mind?

Your account is restorable for 30 days, just sign back in and choose Restore account. After 30 days it's deleted for good and can't be recovered.

Delete by request

If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll handle it. We may ask for a detail or two to confirm the account is yours before we delete it.

Email a deletion request

Send to support@hileonapp.com with the subject "Delete my Active Go account", and include the email or username on the account.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes the data tied to it, including:

  • Your login and profile (email, username, display name, bio, photo, date of birth).
  • Your posts, comments, photos and videos.
  • Activities you created and communities you own.
  • Your messages, connections and followers.
  • Tickets, storefront items, and your subscription records held in the app.
  • Any verification documents you submitted.

What we keep

After deletion we may retain a limited amount of information where we're required to, for example records of purchases needed for tax or accounting, or data kept briefly to protect against fraud and abuse. We may also keep data in an anonymized or aggregated form that no longer identifies you. Purchases themselves are managed by Google Play and are also subject to Google's policies.

Timing

  • Right away: your account and content are hidden and you're signed out.
  • Within 30 days: you can restore the account by signing back in.
  • After 30 days: the account and its data are permanently deleted.
  • Email requests: completed within 30 days of us confirming the account is yours.

Questions? Contact support@hileonapp.com.
