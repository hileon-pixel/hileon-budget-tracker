Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 June 2026

Active Go is an app for finding and joining real-world activities near you. This policy explains what information Active Go collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Active Go is operated by Hileon ("we", "us").

By using Active Go you agree to this policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text), plus a username and display name.
  • Profile: anything you add to your profile, such as a bio, a profile photo, your date of birth (used to confirm you're old enough to use Active Go), and optionally your gender. Your account type, personal, professional or business, is also stored.
  • Content you create: the activities, posts, comments, photos, videos, communities and messages you make or send.
  • Verification documents: if you apply for a professional or business account, the documents you submit to verify it. These are stored privately and are not shown to other users.
  • Purchases: if you buy a subscription, an ad package or a promotion, the purchase is handled by Google Play. We receive a record of what you bought and your subscription status. We never receive or store your card or banking details.

Information we collect automatically

  • Location: with your permission, your device's location, used to show activities near you on the map and to set the location of activities you create. See Location data for detail.
  • Device and technical data: your device type, operating system, app version, and a push-notification token so we can send notifications you've enabled.
  • Usage data: how you use the app, and basic view and reach counts for promoted posts and storefront items, so the hosts and businesses who post them can see simple reach statistics.

How we use your information

  • Run the core service: show nearby activities, let you create and join them, post, message, and take part in communities.
  • Create and maintain your account and keep you signed in.
  • Send notifications you've turned on, such as join requests, approvals, comments and messages.
  • Keep Active Go safe: enforce our rules, review reports, prevent abuse, and check that users meet the minimum age.
  • Process subscriptions, ad packages and promotions.
  • Show hosts and businesses basic reach statistics for content they promote.
  • Fix problems, understand how the app is used, and improve it.
  • Meet legal obligations.

How your information is shared

With other people on Active Go

Your profile and the content you choose to share are visible to others based on the audience you set. People you connect with, communities you join, and activities you take part in will see the information relevant to that. Anyone can be reported or blocked.

With service providers

  • Supabase provides our backend: the database, sign-in, and storage for photos and videos.
  • Google: Google Play processes purchases; Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications; and Google services help display maps and turn coordinates into readable place names.

These providers handle data on our behalf and under their own privacy terms.

For legal and safety reasons

We may share information when the law requires it, to protect the safety of people, or to investigate and act on violations of our rules.

We don't sell your data

We do not sell your personal information, and Active Go does not show third-party ads that track you across other apps.

Location data

Location is central to Active Go, so it gets special care:

  • We only use location after you grant permission, and you can turn it off in your device settings at any time.
  • It's used to show activities near you and to place activities you create on the map.
  • Exact locations are protected. For an activity, other users see only an approximate area until the host accepts them. The precise meeting point is shared once you're in.

Storage and retention

  • Your information is stored on secure infrastructure provided by Supabase.
  • We keep it for as long as your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, it is deactivated straight away and permanently deleted after a 30-day grace period, during which you can restore it by signing back in. After that, your content is removed. We may keep limited records where the law requires, or in anonymized form that no longer identifies you.

Your rights and choices

  • Access and update: view and edit your profile and content in the app.
  • Control your audience: choose who can see your posts, and block anyone you don't want to interact with.
  • Report content: report any profile, post, activity or message.
  • Notifications: manage what you receive in the app's settings and on your device.
  • Delete and restore: delete your account at any time, and restore it within 30 days. See below.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your data, or to object to certain uses. To exercise any of these, contact us at support@hileonapp.com.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and data in two ways: in the app under Settings → Delete account, or from the web if you can't access the app.

Delete your data

Full instructions, what's removed, and a web request form are on our data and account deletion page.

Security

We protect your data with measures including row-level database security so people can only reach data they're allowed to, encryption of data in transit, hashed passwords, a temporary lock after repeated failed sign-in attempts, and private storage for verification documents. No app or service can promise perfect security, but we work to keep your information safe and to respond quickly if something goes wrong.

Children

Active Go is for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children under 16, and we ask for date of birth at sign-up. If we learn that someone under 16 has created an account, we will remove it.

Third-party services

Active Go relies on these services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase, for hosting, authentication and storage.
  • Google Play, for purchases and subscriptions.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging, for push notifications.
  • Google Maps and geocoding, for the map and place names.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the date at the top, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app. Continuing to use Active Go after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at support@hileonapp.com and we'll help.
