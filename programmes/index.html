Programmes put money in people's hands. Keeping track of it is left to memory.

These are the six things participants say most often, and what Hileon does about each one.

"The money is finished before the next payment."A monthly budget, a limit for each category, and a warning while there is still time to adjust.
"I can't say where it went."Spending is logged in seconds, or read from a photo of the receipt, and shown by category and by month.
"The shop's money and the home's money are mixed."Separate Personal and Business books. The business shows its own profit.
"I keep stock, credit and debts in my head."Stock counts with low-stock alerts, invoices, and money lent or borrowed with each repayment.
"Nobody knows what the group's money paid for."A project has a budget, phases and a record of every contribution and cost.
"Notebooks get lost, and data costs money."Everything is kept on the phone, works offline and can be locked with a PIN or fingerprint.

Personal

A household budget that warns early, in the person's own language and currency.

A budget for each monthSet once, or change it month by month when income changes.
Limits by categoryThe bar turns amber as a limit gets close.
Log an expense in secondsAmount, category, done. Twelve everyday categories with clear icons.
History that can be searchedEvery entry by day, with search and filters. Anything can be edited or removed.
Money lent and borrowedWho owes what, with each repayment recorded.
A daily reminder and a streakA gentle nudge to log each day, so the habit sticks after training ends.
Insights in plain wordsA spending health score, this month against last, busiest days, and where the money goes.
Regular paymentsRent and school fees entered once, with the days until each is due.
Receipt scanningPhotograph a till slip and the items, prices and date are filled in.
Any periodSee a month, a quarter, a year or a chosen range, with clear charts for each.

Business

For a shop, a market stall or a farm: sales, costs, stock and profit, kept apart from household money.

Profit at a glanceThe ring shows how much of each sale the business keeps, with net profit for the month.
Sales and invoicesA quick sale in two taps, or a full invoice with the customer's name, paid or unpaid, to share or print.
Stock that counts itselfEach sale reduces stock. Cost, selling price and margin per item, with a low-stock alert.
Costs by typeRent, wages, supplies, transport and more, so the owner sees what is eating the profit.
Tax set asideEnter the local VAT or tax rate once and the app shows how much to reserve.
Scan supplier invoicesTill slips and full A4 invoices are read into separate cost lines.
Recurring costs, with approvalRent and wages are set up once. The app asks before logging each payment.
Business insightsProfit and loss by month, a financial health score, and sales and costs by category.

FullPart of the full app. The free app includes 50 business entries a month.

Project

For money a group manages together: a borehole, a classroom block, a bulk purchase of inputs.

A budget and a deadlineHow much is spent, how much is left and how many days remain.
PhasesSurvey, drilling, pump. Each with its own budget and status.
Every cost on recordLabour, materials, transport, permits, with the supplier's name.
ContributionsWhat each member, household or funder has put in.
Savings goalsA target funded from what is left of the personal budget.
Linked booksProject spending can also show in the Personal or Business book that paid for it.

FullProject mode is part of the full app.
